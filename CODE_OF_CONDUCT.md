# Code of Conduct — CaseTree AI

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the CaseTree AI project a respectful and collaborative experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users (university lecturers and students)
- Showing empathy towards other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional academic setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate.
